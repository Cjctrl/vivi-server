# May 6

May 6 is the 126th day of the year (127th in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar. There are 239 days remaining until the end of the year.

## Historical Events
- **1840**: The Penny Black, the first adhesive postage stamp, becomes valid for use in the United Kingdom
- **1937**: The German airship Hindenburg catches fire and is destroyed during its attempt to dock with its mooring mast at Lakehurst Naval Air Station in New Jersey
- **1994**: The Channel Tunnel (Chunnel) connecting England and France opens for passenger service
- **2004**: The final episode of the television series "Friends" airs

## Observances
- **International No Diet Day** (observed on May 6)
- **National Nurses Day** (United States, observed on May 6 as part of National Nurses Week)

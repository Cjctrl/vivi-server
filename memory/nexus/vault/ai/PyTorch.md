**PyTorch** is an [open-source](Open_source "Open source") [deep learning](Deep_learning "Deep learning") [library](Library_\(computing\) "Library \(computing\)"), originally developed by [Meta Platforms](Meta_Platforms "Meta Platforms") and currently developed with support from the [Linux Foundation](Linux_Foundation "Linux Foundation"). The successor to [Torch](Torch_\(machine_learning\) "Torch \(machine learning\)"), PyTorch provides a [high-level](Abstraction_\(computer_science\) "Abstraction \(computer science\)") [API](Application_programming_interface "Application programming interface") that builds upon optimised, low-level implementations of deep learning algorithms and architectures, such as the [Transformer](Transformer_\(deep_learning\) "Transformer \(deep learning\)"), or [SGD](Stochastic_gradient_descent "Stochastic gradient descent"). Notably, this API simplifies model [training](Machine_learning#Approaches "Machine learning") and inference to a few lines of code. PyTorch allows for automatic [parallelization](Parallelization "Parallelization") of training and, internally, implements [CUDA](CUDA "CUDA") bindings that speed training further by [leveraging GPU resources](General-purpose_computing_on_graphics_processing_units "General-purpose computing on graphics processing units"). 

PyTorch utilises the [tensor](Tensor_\(machine_learning\) "Tensor \(machine learning\)") as a fundamental [data type](Type_system "Type system"), similarly to [NumPy](NumPy "NumPy"). Training is facilitated by a [reversed automatic differentiation](Automatic_differentiation#Reverse_accumulation "Automatic differentiation") system, Autograd, that constructs a [directed acyclic graph](Directed_acyclic_graph "Directed acyclic graph") of the operations (and their arguments) executed by a model during its forward pass. With a [loss](Loss_\(machine_learning\) "Loss \(machine learning\)"), [backpropagation](Backpropagation "Backpropagation") is then undertaken. 

As of 2025, PyTorch remains one of the most popular deep learning libraries, alongside others such as [TensorFlow](TensorFlow "TensorFlow") and [Keras](Keras "Keras"). A number of commercial deep learning architectures are built on top of PyTorch, including [ChatGPT](ChatGPT "ChatGPT"), [Tesla Autopilot](Tesla_Autopilot "Tesla Autopilot"), [Uber](Uber "Uber")'s Pyro, [Hugging Face](Hugging_Face "Hugging Face")'s Transformers, and Catalyst.

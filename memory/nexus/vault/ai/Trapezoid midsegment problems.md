The midsegment formula: midsegment = (base1 + base2) / 2

So VS = (QR + UT) / 2

1. QR = 12, UT = 22: VS = (12 + 22)/2 = 17
2. VS = 9, UT = 12: 9 = (QR + 12)/2 → 18 = QR + 12 → QR = 6
3. QR = 2, VS = 7: 7 = (2 + UT)/2 → 14 = 2 + UT → UT = 12## Trapezoid Midsegment Problems

**VS** is the midsegment of trapezoid QRTU (connecting the midpoints of the two legs). The midsegment formula is:

$$VS = \frac{QR + UT}{2}$$

---

### Problem 1 — Find VS
> QR = 12, UT = 22

$$VS = \frac{12 + 22}{2} = \frac{34}{2} = \boxed{17}$$

---

### Problem 2 — Find QR
> VS = 9, UT = 12

$$9 = \frac{QR + 12}{2}$$
$$18 = QR + 12$$
$$QR = \boxed{6}$$

---

### Problem 3 — Find UT
> QR = 2, VS = 7

$$7 = \frac{2 + UT}{2}$$
$$14 = 2 + UT$$
$$UT = \boxed{12}$$

---

**The key rule:** The midsegment always equals the **average of the two bases**. So you can always solve for the missing value by plugging into that formula and doing basic algebra.

**TensorFlow** is a [software library](Library_\(computing\) "Library \(computing\)") for [machine learning](Machine_learning "Machine learning") and [artificial intelligence](Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence"). It can be used across a range of tasks, but is used mainly for [training](Types_of_artificial_neural_networks#Training "Types of artificial neural networks") and [inference](Statistical_inference "Statistical inference") of [neural networks](Neural_network_\(machine_learning\) "Neural network \(machine learning\)"). It is one of the most popular [deep learning](Deep_learning "Deep learning") frameworks, alongside others such as [PyTorch](PyTorch "PyTorch"). It is [free and open-source software](Free_and_open-source_software "Free and open-source software") released under the [Apache License 2.0](Apache_License_2.0 "Apache License 2.0"). 

It was developed by the [Google Brain](Google_Brain "Google Brain") team for [Google](Google "Google")'s internal use in research and production. The initial version was released under the [Apache License 2.0](Apache_License_2.0 "Apache License 2.0") in 2015. Google released an updated version, TensorFlow 2.0, in September 2019. 

TensorFlow can be used in a wide variety of programming languages, including [Python](Python_\(programming_language\) "Python \(programming language\)"), [JavaScript](JavaScript "JavaScript"), [C++](C%2B%2B "C++"), and [Java](Java_\(programming_language\) "Java \(programming language\)"), facilitating its use in a range of applications in many sectors.

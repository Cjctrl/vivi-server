* **What is the first reference to Jesus and Mary in the Bible? What is the context in which they appear? What is promised?** The first reference is in Genesis 3:15 with Adam and Eve. God promises that Eve's offspring will defeat Satan and redeem us from sin.
* **What are two examples of miraculous births that occurred in the Old Testament?** Isaac and Samuel.
* **Who were Mary's parents?** Joachim and Ann, who were descendants of David.
* **The New Testament calls Jesus the new Adam. Why would Mary be called the new Eve?** Mary said yes to God where Eve said no, making up for Eve's disobedience.
* **What does the Virgin Birth tell us about the Person of Jesus?** It shows Jesus has no human father and is truly God.
* **Why would God desire that Mary choose to cooperate with this plan?** Because Eve freely chose to disobey God, He wanted a woman to freely choose to obey Him.
* **What is meant by divine motherhood?** Mary is the Mother of God.
* **How can Mary be the Mother of God?** She is the mother of Jesus, who is both fully human and fully divine.
* **How do the words of St. Elizabeth show us that Mary is the Mother of God?** Elizabeth calls Mary "the mother of my Lord."
* **Mary said, "All generations will call me blessed." What prayer fulfills the words of Mary?** The Hail Mary.
* **What is meant by the Immaculate Conception? How does the greeting of St. Gabriel confirm this belief?** Mary was conceived without original sin. Gabriel calls her "full of grace," confirming this.
* **The Church defined the Dogma of the Immaculate Conception in 1854. Does this mean that the belief was not held before that year? Explain.** No, Christians believed this before 1854. The Church made it official teaching in 1854.
* **How did the apparition of the Blessed Virgin Mary at Lourdes confirm the Church's solemn Definition of the Dogma of the Immaculate Conception?** Mary called herself "the Immaculate Conception."
* **What does the Perpetual Virginity of Mary signify?** Mary remained a virgin her entire life.
* **What does the Gospel mean when it refers to the brothers of Our Lord?** They were relatives or close family members, not biological brothers of Jesus.
* **How do the words of Christ to St. John at the Crucifixion support the belief in the perpetual virginity of Mary?** Jesus gave Mary to John's care, showing she had no other children to care for her.
* **What is the Assumption? What other person(s) are believed to have been assumed into Heaven? Why is the Assumption a natural consequence of the Immaculate Conception?** Mary was taken body and soul into Heaven. Enoch and Elijah were also assumed. Since Mary was without sin, her body didn't need to decay.
* **What is meant when Mary is referred to as the Ark of the Covenant?** Mary carried Jesus (the new covenant) just as the ark carried God's presence.
* **How can Mary be "Our Mother"?** She's our spiritual mother because she's the mother of Jesus, and we are part of Christ's body, the Church.
* **Twice in the Gospels Jesus refers to Mary as "woman." What does he mean by this term?** Jesus was calling her the "new Eve."
* **Jesus asks St. John to take care of Mary. What else is intended in this statement? (Refer to your answer in number 19.)** Jesus was giving Mary to all of us as our spiritual mother whom we can ask for prayers.
* **How does Mary fit the description of the Lady clothed with the Sun in the Book of Revelation?** She's the Queen of Heaven whose Son rules all nations, matching the twelve stars and other symbols.
* **In what way is Mary a Mediatrix and a Redemptrix?** She helps bring God's grace to us and participated in our redemption through Christ.
* **List several ways in which Mary is a model for the Christian life.** She said yes to God, lived without sin, trusted God completely, served faithfully, and suffered with Christ.
* **What was the role of St. Joseph in the Holy Family? What is the role in the Catholic Church and in the Christian life?** He was Jesus's foster father and protector. Today he protects the universal Catholic Church.

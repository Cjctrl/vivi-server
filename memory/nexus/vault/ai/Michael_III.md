# Michael III

Michael III (Greek: Μιχαήλ; January 9, 840 – September 23, 867), commonly known as the Drunkard, was Byzantine Emperor from 840 to 867. He was the third and traditionally last member of the Amorian dynasty.

## Reign
- **Accession**: Became emperor at age two following the death of his father, Emperor Theophilos
- **Regency**: Initially ruled under the regency of his mother Theodora and others
- **Major Events**: 
  - End of Byzantine Iconoclasm (843)
  - Continued conflicts with the Abbasid Caliphate
  - Missionary activities among Slavic peoples
  - Internal struggles and court intrigues

## Personal Life
Known for his fondness for alcohol and chariot racing, which contributed to his nickname "the Drunkard"
- Married to the daughter of a prominent naval official
- Faced opposition from various factions within the empire

## Legacy
His reign marked the end of the Amorian dynasty, as he was assassinated by Basil the Macedonian, who founded the Macedonian dynasty that would lead to Byzantine Empire's golden age.

# May 7

May 7 is the 127th day of the year (128th in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar. There are 238 days remaining until the end of the year.

## Historical Events
- **1429**: Joan of Arc lifts the Siege of Orléans, turning the tide of the Hundred Years' War
- **1824**: Beethoven's Ninth Symphony premieres in Vienna
- **1915**: The RMS Lusitania is sunk by a German U-boat, contributing to the United States' entry into World War I
- **1945**: Germany signs an unconditional surrender at Allied headquarters in Reims, France (effective May 8)
- **1992**: The Space Shuttle Endeavour is launched on its first mission (STS-49)
- **2017**: Emmanuel Macron is inaugurated as President of France

## Observances
- None widely recognized international observances on this date

## In Technology and Computing
- **1945**: The surrender of Germany marked the end of World War II in Europe, leading to the beginning of the Cold War era and subsequent technological developments including early computing developments
- **1992**: The Space Shuttle Endeavour mission demonstrated advanced spacecraft technology and robotics in space

In the context of computing history, the post-World War II period saw accelerated development of electronic computers, which laid the foundation for modern computing and eventually artificial intelligence.

### 🤖 Claude · 2025-06-02 13:08

Here are the numbered answers to your questions about Christology:

**1. What are the consequences of denying Jesus was truly human?**
There would have been no Redemption, for there is no forgiveness of sins without the shedding of blood. Without true humanity, Christ could not have truly suffered and died for our sins.

**2. From whom did Christ receive his humanity?**
The Blessed Virgin Mary.

**3. What does the mystery of Christ's conception by the Holy Spirit signify?**
By virtue of her virginal conception, Jesus has no biological father; instead he was sent from his Father in Heaven, signifying his divine origin while maintaining true human nature through Mary.

**4. What are the consequences of denying Jesus was God?**
Those who deny Christ's divinity believe that Christ must have been a superior human being - a great prophet blessed by God, but could not have been of the same nature as God. In response to this heresy, the Church identified Christ as Lord, affirming his divine nature.

**5. What does the Catholic Church teach about Christ's humanity and his divinity?**
Christ was both true God and true Man. He is the ONLY BEGOTTEN SON of the Father who took on human nature in order to bring grace and truth to people.

**6. What is divine filiation?**
The acceptance of a person by God as his child in Baptism.

**7. What does consubstantial mean in relation to Jesus Christ?**
Consubstantial is from the Latin meaning "of the same substance" - Jesus is of the same divine substance as the Father.

**8. What does Logos mean? What does John say about Logos and his relationship to God?**
Logos means "Word" - Jesus is the Word. St. John describes Christ as the Word who has existed for all eternity and who is a Person coexistent with God, for he is God himself.

**9. List three verses of Scripture that attest to Christ's divinity.**
- John 1:1
- John 5:37  
- John 8:28

**10. What is significant about Christ's use of the word "I AM"?**
"I AM" is precisely the divine name that was revealed to Moses; this term is reserved for God alone.

**11. What is hypostatic union?**
The theological term that describes Christ as one Person with two natures, divine and human - the One Person of Jesus Christ.

**12. What does it mean to call Jesus the Christ?**
By calling Jesus "Christ," the Apostles proclaimed he is the promised Messiah (anointed one) foretold by the prophets.

**13. Why did many people reject Jesus as the Messiah? What did they expect?**
They wanted a military leader who would drive the Romans from the Holy Land and reestablish Israel as a great nation.

**14. What kind of kingdom did Jesus come to establish?**
Christ established a Kingdom not of this world but to which everyone may be admitted as a son or daughter of God - this Kingdom is the Church.

**15. Why did Jesus call himself the Son of Man?**
Because Christ has two distinct natures - he is true God and true man.

**16. Why do the enemies of Christianity try to disprove the Resurrection? What does the Resurrection prove?**
The Resurrection proves that Christ has fully risen, confirming his divine nature and the truth of his claims. Enemies try to disprove it because it is the foundation of Christian faith and proof of Christ's victory over death and sin.

### 🧑 CJ · 2025-06-02 13:17

What did Jesus do during the forty days following his Resurrection?
Christ appeared to his disciples, spoke about the Kingdom of God, and prepared them for their public ministries.
What did he tell his Apostles immediately before his Ascension into Heaven?
"You shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria and to the end of the earth"
According to John 14, how would the Holy Spirit help the Apostles?
Christ told his Apostles, "The Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you"
What did the Apostles do between the Ascension and Pentecost?
They returned to Jerusalem and, in the Upper Room, devoted themselves to the prayers
Why did the eleven elect another Apostle? Who made this decision?
St. Peter decided the Apostles needed to fill the office Judas had vacated.
What does the word "office" mean in the context of Acts 1? What does this tell is about the successors of the Apostles in the Church today?
In Greek, the office is episcope, from which comes episcopacy and in English bishop. Every bishop is, by his office, a successor to the Apostles due to the unbroken succession of bishops from the Apostles.
What effect did the Holy Spirit have on the Apostles at Pentecost? What does it tell us about the role of the Holy Spirit in the Church today?
The Holy Spirit gave the Apostles both the courage to preach the Gospel and the ability to proclaim it so it could be understood and accepted. Members of the Church today must also rely on the Holy Spirit for everything they need to live out their vocations.
What do the episodes of the Visitation (St. Elizabeth) and the Presentation (Simeon) reveal about the role of the Holy Spirit in the Christian life?
The Visitation and Presentation reveal the Holy Spirit opens people's eyes to God's presence in the world.
When does a Christian normally receive the gift of the Holy Spirit?
The Christian receives a special outpouring of the Holy Spirit in the Sacrament of Confirmation, usually administered after having reached the ra in the Latin Rite.
How was the gift of the Holy Spirit conveyed in the early Church?
It was conveyed by the laying on of hands.
What role did the Holy Spirit have in the writing of Sacred Scripture?
The Holy Spirit is the ultimate author of the Sacred Scriptures, inspiring the Sacred Authors to communicate what God wanted and nothing more.
In what way is Sacred Scripture like the Person of Christ?
Like Christ, the inspired books are in a sense human and divine. In Christ, the Word of God took on human flesh to become a true man so people could encounter God. In the Scriptures, the Word of God takes on the weakness of human language so people can understand it.
What are different meanings of the Paraclete?
It can be translated Counselor, Teacher, Advocate, Intercessor, Helper, and Comforter.
What are the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit?
They are faith, hope, and charity (love). Their object is God, such that people can believe what God has revealed, have confidence his promises will be fulfilled, and act according to his will.
What are the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit?
They are wisdom, understanding, counsel, fortitude, knowledge, piety, and fear of the Lord.
List the twelve fruits of the Holy Spirit.
They are love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, generosity, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control, modesty, chastity, and goodness.
What are the symbols of the Holy Spirit? Choose three of them and explain their significance.
They are water, anointing, fire, cloud and light, a hand, and a dove. In Baptism, the Holy Spirit cleanses and gives new life. Fire represents courage and purification. A dove represents peace and purity of heart.

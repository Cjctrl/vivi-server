**CubicWeb** is a [free and open-source](Free_and_open-source "Free and open-source") semantic [web application framework](Web_application_framework "Web application framework"), licensed under the LGPL. It is written in [Python](Python_\(programming_language\) "Python \(programming language\)"). 

It has been an open free software project since October 2008, but the project began in 2000, and was initially developed by Logilab for internal uses such as [intranet](Intranet "Intranet"), [bug tracker](Bug_tracker "Bug tracker") and forge applications. 

As of 2012, CubicWeb is being used in large-scale [semantic web](Semantic_web "Semantic web") and [linked open data](Linked_open_data "Linked open data") applications, and international corporations.

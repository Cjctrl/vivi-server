# Lauren Bacall

Lauren Bacall (born Betty Joan Perske; September 16, 1924 – August 12, 2014) was an American actress known for her distinctive voice and sultry looks. She emerged as a leading lady in Hollywood during the 1940s and continued her career in film, television, and theater for several decades.

## Career Highlights
- **Breakthrough Role**: "To Have and Have Not" (1944) with Humphrey Bogart
- **Notable Films**: "The Big Sleep", "Dark Passage", "Key Largo", "Written on the Wind"
- **Awards**: Tony Award, honorary Oscar, Golden Globe nominations
- **Broadway Success**: Notable performances in various stage productions

## Personal Life
- Married to Humphrey Bogart (1945-1957)
- Later married to Jason Robards (1961-1969)
- Children: Stephen Bogart, Leslie Robards

Glossary

  * [Glossary](Glossary_of_artificial_intelligence "Glossary of artificial intelligence")

  
  
In [machine learning](Machine_learning "Machine learning"), **deep learning** (**DL**) focuses on utilizing multilayered [neural networks](Neural_network_\(machine_learning\) "Neural network \(machine learning\)") to perform tasks such as [classification](Statistical_classification "Statistical classification"), [regression](Regression_analysis "Regression analysis"), and [representation learning](Representation_learning "Representation learning"). The field takes inspiration from [biological neuroscience](Neuroscience "Neuroscience") and revolves around stacking [artificial neurons](Artificial_neuron "Artificial neuron") into layers and "training" them to process data. The adjective "deep" refers to the use of multiple layers (ranging from three to several hundred or thousands) in the network. Methods used can be [supervised](Supervised_learning "Supervised learning"), [semi-supervised](Semi-supervised_learning "Semi-supervised learning") or [unsupervised](Unsupervised_learning "Unsupervised learning"). 

Some common deep learning network architectures include [fully connected networks](Fully_connected_network "Fully connected network"), [deep belief networks](Deep_belief_network "Deep belief network"), [recurrent neural networks](Recurrent_neural_network "Recurrent neural network"), [convolutional neural networks](Convolutional_neural_network "Convolutional neural network"), [generative adversarial networks](Generative_adversarial_network "Generative adversarial network"), [transformers](Transformer_\(deep_learning\) "Transformer \(deep learning\)"), and [neural radiance fields](Neural_radiance_field "Neural radiance field"). These architectures have been applied to fields including [computer vision](Computer_vision "Computer vision"), [speech recognition](Speech_recognition "Speech recognition"), [natural language processing](Natural_language_processing "Natural language processing"), [machine translation](Machine_translation "Machine translation"), [bioinformatics](Bioinformatics "Bioinformatics"), [drug design](Drug_design "Drug design"), [medical image analysis](Medical_image_analysis "Medical image analysis"), [climate science](Climatology "Climatology"), material inspection and [board game](Board_game "Board game") programs, where they have produced results comparable to and in some cases surpassing human expert performance. 

Early forms of neural networks were inspired by information processing and distributed communication nodes in [biological systems](Biological_system "Biological system"), particularly the [human brain](Human_brain "Human brain"). However, current neural networks do not intend to model the brain function of organisms, and are generally seen as low-quality models for that purpose.

# Neural network

For other uses, see [Neural network (disambiguation)](Neural_network_\(disambiguation\) "Neural network \(disambiguation\)").

A **neural network** is a group of interconnected units called [neurons](Neurons "Neurons") that send signals to one another. Neurons can be either [biological cells](Cell_\(biology\) "Cell \(biology\)") or [mathematical models](Mathematical_model "Mathematical model"). While individual neurons are simple, many of them together in a network can perform complex tasks. There are two main types of neural networks. 

  * In [neuroscience](Neuroscience "Neuroscience"), a _[biological neural network](Biological_neural_network "Biological neural network")_ is a physical structure found in [brains](Brain "Brain") and complex [nervous systems](Nervous_system "Nervous system") – a population of nerve cells connected by [synapses](Synapse "Synapse").
  * In [machine learning](Machine_learning "Machine learning"), an _[artificial neural network](Neural_network_\(machine_learning\) "Neural network \(machine learning\)")_ is a mathematical model used to approximate [nonlinear functions](Nonlinear_function "Nonlinear function"). Artificial neural networks are used to solve [artificial intelligence](Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") problems.

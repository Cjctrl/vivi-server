# May 8

May 8 is the 128th day of the year (129th in leap years) in the Gregorian calendar. There are 237 days remaining until the end of the year.

## Historical Events
- **1429**: Joan of Arc lifts the Siege of Orléans, turning the tide of the Hundred Years' War
- **1846**: Mexican-American War: The Battle of Palo Alto results in the first major U.S. victory
- **1945**: Victory in Europe Day (V-E Day) - World War II ends in Europe with Germany's surrender
- **1973**: A 71-day standoff between federal authorities and the American Indian Movement activists occupying the Pine Ridge Reservation at Wounded Knee ends
- **2020**: The first confirmed case of COVID-19 in Latin America is reported in Brazil

## Observances
- **Victory in Europe Day** (V-E Day) - Commemorates the end of World War II in Europe
- **World Red Cross and Red Crescent Day** (observed on May 8)
- **World Thalassemia Day** (observed on May 8)

## In Technology and Computing
- **1945**: The end of World War II in Europe marked a turning point in technological development, leading to increased investment in research and development, including early computing projects that were developed during the war (such as code-breaking machines like Colossus and ENIAC)
- The post-war period saw the beginning of the computer age, with many wartime technologies being adapted for civilian use

In the context of artificial intelligence, the developments in computing following World War II laid the groundwork for the field of AI, which was formally established as an academic discipline at the Dartmouth Workshop in 1956.

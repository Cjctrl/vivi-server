**NetworkX** is a [Python](Python_\(programming_language\) "Python \(programming language\)") library for studying [graphs](Graph_\(discrete_mathematics\) "Graph \(discrete mathematics\)") and [networks](Network_theory "Network theory"). NetworkX is [free software](Free_software "Free software") released under the [BSD-new license](BSD-new "BSD-new").

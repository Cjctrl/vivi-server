Here's how to download all your Claude chats:

**The official built-in export** is the easiest way to get everything at once:

1. Click your **initials** in the lower-left corner of claude.ai
2. Select **Settings**
3. Go to the **Privacy** section
4. Click **Export data**
5. You'll receive a download link via email once the export is processed — check the inbox associated with your account.

The download arrives as a ZIP file with your conversations stored in JSON format. JSON isn't the prettiest to read directly, but you can open it in a text editor like VS Code, or paste it into a free online JSON viewer.

A few things to note:
- This only works on the **web app or Claude Desktop** — you can't trigger an export from the iOS or Android app.
- Claude exports all your data at once; you can't selectively export individual conversations this way.

**If you want individual chats in a nicer format** (PDF, Markdown, etc.), browser extensions like *AI Chat Exporter* or *Claude Exporter* are popular options available for Chrome and Firefox. These let you export one conversation at a time in more readable formats.

### 🤖 Claude · 2025-06-02 12:50

Here are the answers written as a high school student would respond:

**Some heresies deny that Jesus was truly human. What are the consequences of this belief?**
If Jesus wasn't truly human, then he couldn't have really died for our sins. The consequence is that there would be no real redemption because only a true human death could pay for human sin. Without his humanity, his sacrifice wouldn't count.

**From whom did Christ receive his humanity?**
Jesus received his humanity from the Blessed Virgin Mary. Since he was conceived by the Holy Spirit, he doesn't have a biological father - his Father is God in Heaven. The mystery of his conception shows that he's both divine and human.

**Other heresies deny that Jesus was God. What are the consequences of this belief?**
If Jesus wasn't God, then he would just be a really good human teacher or prophet, but not actually divine. This means he couldn't save us from sin because only God has the power to forgive sins and give eternal life. He'd be impressive but not our Savior.

**What does the Catholic Church teach about Christ's humanity and his divinity?**
The Catholic Church teaches that Christ is both true God and true man at the same time. He's the only Son of the Father who became human to bring us grace and truth. He has two complete natures in one person.

**What is divine filiation?**
Divine filiation is when God accepts someone as his child through Baptism. It's how we become adopted sons and daughters of God.

**What does consubstantial mean in relation to Jesus Christ?**
Consubstantial means "of the same substance." So Jesus is of the same substance as the Father - they're both truly God, not just similar to God.

**What does Logos mean? What does John say about Logos and his relationship to God?**
Logos means "Word." John says that Jesus is the Word who has existed forever with God and actually is God. The Word became flesh and lived among us.

**List three verses of Scripture that attest to Christ's divinity.**
- John 1:1 - "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God"
- John 5:37 - References the Father bearing witness to Christ
- John 8:28 - Jesus speaks of his divine relationship with the Father

**What is significant about Christ's use of the word, "I AM?"**
When Jesus says "I AM," he's using the same name God gave to Moses at the burning bush. This name is reserved only for God, so Jesus is claiming to be God himself.

**What is hypostatic union?**
The hypostatic union is the theological term that describes how Jesus Christ is one person with two complete natures - divine and human. He's fully God and fully man at the same time.

**What does it mean to call Jesus the Christ?**
Calling Jesus "Christ" means calling him the Messiah or "anointed one" that the Old Testament prophets promised would come. It's recognizing him as the Savior.

**Why did many people reject Jesus as the Messiah? What did they expect?**
Many people rejected Jesus because they expected the Messiah to be a military leader who would kick out the Romans and make Israel a powerful nation again. Jesus didn't fit their political expectations.

**What kind of kingdom did Jesus come to establish?**
Jesus came to establish a spiritual kingdom, not an earthly political one. His kingdom is the Church, where everyone can become sons and daughters of God. It's "not of this world."

**Why did Jesus call himself the Son of Man?**
Jesus called himself the Son of Man to emphasize his human nature while also referring to the divine figure from Daniel's prophecy. It shows he has both divine and human natures.

**Why do the enemies of Christianity try to disprove the Resurrection? What does the Resurrection prove?**
Enemies try to disprove the Resurrection because if Jesus really rose from the dead, it proves everything he said about being God is true. The Resurrection proves that Christ conquered death and sin, and that he really is divine.

### 🤖 Claude · 2025-06-01 15:27

I'll help you with these important questions about female reproductive health. Let me address each one:

**1. Which organ transports eggs from the ovaries to the uterus?**
The **fallopian tubes** transport eggs from the ovaries to the uterus. These tubes have tiny hair-like structures called cilia that help move the egg along the pathway.

**2. Why is the vagina sometimes called the birth canal?**
The vagina is called the birth canal because it's the passageway through which a baby travels during childbirth when leaving the uterus and entering the world.

**3. What change in the body causes menstruation?**
Menstruation occurs when hormone levels (especially estrogen and progesterone) drop, causing the lining of the uterus (endometrium) to shed. This happens when pregnancy doesn't occur during that cycle.

**4. What is the difference between ovarian cyst and ovarian cancer?**
- **Ovarian cysts** are usually fluid-filled sacs that are often benign (non-cancerous) and may go away on their own
- **Ovarian cancer** involves malignant (cancerous) cells that grow uncontrollably and can spread to other parts of the body

**5. Why should females wipe from front to back after urinating?**
Wiping from front to back prevents bacteria from the anal area from being transferred to the urethra and vagina, which helps prevent urinary tract infections (UTIs) and other infections.

**6. What kind of doctor specializes in female reproductive health?**
An **obstetrician-gynecologist (OB-GYN)** specializes in female reproductive health. They handle both obstetrics (pregnancy and childbirth) and gynecology (female reproductive system health).

These are all important aspects of understanding female reproductive health and hygiene!

## DEFINITIONS

- **Nucleotide** – The basic building block of DNA; made of a sugar, phosphate group, and nitrogenous base
- **Restriction enzyme** – An enzyme that cuts DNA at specific recognition sequences
- **Electrophoresis** – A technique using electric current to separate DNA fragments by size through a gel
- **Polymerase** – An enzyme that builds new DNA strands (key in PCR)
- **CODIS** – Combined DNA Index System; the FBI's national DNA database for matching profiles
- **Gary Ridgeway** – The "Green River Killer," convicted using DNA evidence
- **Amplification** – Making many copies of a DNA segment (what PCR does)
- **Loci** – Specific locations on a chromosome where a gene/marker is found

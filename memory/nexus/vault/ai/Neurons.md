# Neuron

A neuron is a specialized cell that transmits nerve impulses; a nerve cell is also known as a neuron. Neurons are the basic working units of the brain and nervous system, responsible for receiving sensory input from the external world, sending motor commands to our muscles, and transforming and relaying the electrical signals at every step in between.

In neuroscience, a biological neural network is a population of neurons connected by synapses. In machine learning, an artificial neural network is a mathematical model based on biological neural networks, used to approximate complex functions and learn from data.

## Biological Neurons
Biological neurons consist of:
- **Soma (cell body)**: Contains the nucleus and carries out metabolic functions
- **Dendrites**: Branch-like structures that receive signals from other neurons
- **Axon**: Long projection that transmits signals away from the cell body
- **Synapses**: Junctions where neurons communicate with each other

## Artificial Neurons
In machine learning, artificial neurons (or perceptrons) are mathematical functions that:
- Receive weighted inputs
- Apply an activation function
- Produce an output

Common activation functions include:
- Sigmoid
- ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit)
- Tanh
- Softmax

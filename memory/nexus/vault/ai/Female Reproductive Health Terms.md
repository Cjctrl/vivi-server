### 🤖 Claude · 2025-06-01 15:28

Here are clear definitions for these women's health and reproductive system terms:

**Breast cancer** - A type of cancer that forms in breast tissue, most commonly in the ducts that carry milk or the lobules that produce milk.

**Breast** - Mammary glands that produce milk for breastfeeding, composed of fatty tissue, ducts, and lobules.

**Cervix** - The narrow, lower portion of the uterus that connects to the vagina and serves as the opening to the uterus.

**Clitoris** - A sensitive organ located at the top of the vulva, containing thousands of nerve endings and playing a key role in sexual pleasure.

**Eggs (Ova)** - Female reproductive cells produced by the ovaries that can be fertilized by sperm to create pregnancy.

**Endometriosis** - A condition where tissue similar to the uterine lining grows outside the uterus, causing pain and potentially affecting fertility.

**Fallopian tubes** - Two tubes that connect the ovaries to the uterus, where fertilization typically occurs and eggs travel to the uterus.

**Female reproductive system** - The organs involved in reproduction, including ovaries, fallopian tubes, uterus, cervix, and vagina.

**Fibroids** - Non-cancerous growths in or on the uterus that can cause heavy bleeding, pain, or pressure symptoms.

**Labia** - The folds of skin surrounding the vaginal opening, including the outer labia majora and inner labia minora.

**Menstrual cycle** - The monthly process where the body prepares for potential pregnancy, typically lasting 21-35 days.

**Menstruation** - The monthly shedding of the uterine lining when pregnancy doesn't occur, resulting in bleeding that lasts 3-7 days.

**Obstetrician-gynecologist (OB-GYN)** - A doctor specializing in women's reproductive health, pregnancy, and childbirth.

**Ovarian cyst** - Fluid-filled sacs that develop on or in the ovaries, often harmless but sometimes requiring medical attention.

**Ovaries** - Two small organs that produce eggs and hormones like estrogen and progesterone.

**Ovulation** - The release of a mature egg from the ovary, typically occurring mid-cycle around day 14.

**Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)** - Physical and emotional symptoms that occur before menstruation, including mood changes, bloating, and breast tenderness.

**Toxic shock syndrome (TSS)** - A rare but serious bacterial infection that can be associated with tampon use or other causes.

**Uterus** - The hollow muscular organ where a fertilized egg implants and a fetus develops during pregnancy.

**Vagina** - The muscular canal connecting the external genitals to the cervix, serving as the birth canal and menstrual flow pathway.

### 🤖 Claude · 2025-06-01 15:36

Here are the answers to your questions:

**1. Which organ transports?**
The fallopian tubes transport eggs from the ovaries to the uterus.

**2. Why is the vagina sometimes called the birth canal?**
The vagina is called the birth canal because it is the passageway through which a baby travels during childbirth to exit the mother's body.

**3. What change in the body causes menstruation?**
The drop in hormone levels (estrogen and progesterone) causes the thick lining of the uterus (endometrium) to shed, resulting in menstrual bleeding.

**4. What is the difference between ovarian cyst and ovarian cancer?**
- **Ovarian cyst:** A fluid-filled sac that is usually benign (non-cancerous) and often goes away on its own
- **Ovarian cancer:** Malignant (cancerous) cells that grow uncontrollably and can spread to other parts of the body

**5. Why should females wipe from front to back after urinating?**
Wiping from front to back prevents bacteria from the anal area from being transferred to the urethra and vagina, which helps prevent urinary tract infections and other infections.

**6. What kind of doctor specializes in female reproductive health?**
An obstetrician-gynecologist (OB-GYN) specializes in female reproductive health, including pregnancy, childbirth, and disorders of the reproductive system.
